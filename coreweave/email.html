Great to hear from you, and glad it's fun. Not surprised, if Mike's got the gang back together.

I'm in Ceará at the moment, right next to ByteDance's build at Pecém — R$150bn, 1.5GW, live 2027, the largest complex they've put anywhere outside China. What strikes you standing here is how much room is left: nine projects are already approved across the port complex, roughly R$558bn in total, and it isn't close to full.

The national picture is the same shape. Scala has 4.75GW authorised for its AI City campus in Rio Grande do Sul, a ~US$50bn build, and about R$500bn has been announced country-wide. FGV Projetos sizes the headroom at +12.7GW by 2035 — from roughly 1GW today to 13.7 — worth US$432–698bn, though worth knowing Scala commissioned that one.

Three things make Ceará work specifically:

Power. Casa dos Ventos is supplying ByteDance 100% renewable from new wind in Ceará and Piauí — 2.1GW installed. Brazil's grid is close to 90% renewable overall.

Connectivity. 16 submarine cables land in Ceará with another in build, giving direct routes to North America, Europe and Africa. That's the real reason Pecém won, not just the wind.

Tax. Data centres inside ZPE Ceará already get a special tariff regime, conditioned on running exclusively renewable. On top of that, REDATA — a five-year federal tax suspension on equipment — passed the Chamber in February and is in front of the Senate now. I've been following the vote.

I mentioned it because I saw CoreWeave has started expanding internationally — Europe, and now Indonesia. From where I'm sitting, Brazil looks like it belongs on that list, and the window while REDATA is still being decided is probably the interesting one.

Forward it on if it's useful to whoever's looking at the region.
